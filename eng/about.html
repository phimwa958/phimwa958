About Me

Hello, I'm a Developer Driven by Curiosity.

I am a final-year Computer Science student at the Faculty of Science and Technology, Suan Dusit University, with a deep-seated passion for both building elegant web applications and uncovering stories within data. My journey into the world of technology began with a simple line of Python code and has since evolved into a path where I can architect, build, and deploy full-stack applications.

My Journey

Starting with foundational Python exercises, I quickly found myself drawn to the creative possibilities of web development. I dove into Django, building projects that taught me the fundamentals of backend architecture. This led me to explore the frontend with React and Node.js, allowing me to build complete, end-to-end systems. My curiosity didn't stop there; seeing the power of data in my web apps sparked a fascination with artificial intelligence. Recently, during my internships, I expanded my expertise into serverless cloud architectures and successfully integrated Large Language Models (LLMs) into production systems to automate complex text processing workflows. I also gained hands-on experience in building CI/CD pipelines and optimizing data engineering processes.

My Philosophy

I believe in the power of clean, maintainable code and the importance of continuous learning. Technology is always evolving, and I strive to stay current with the latest trends and best practices. I approach every project not just as a task to be completed, but as an opportunity to learn, grow, and create something of value.

Beyond the Code

When I'm not at my computer, I enjoy exploring emerging technologies, studying new frameworks to stay ahead of the curve, and listening to music. These activities help me stay balanced and often provide unexpected inspiration for my next project.
